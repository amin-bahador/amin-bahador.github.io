Doctoral research · Italy · In progress

Road and landscape
as territorial systems

My doctoral project investigates roads as territorial and perceptual systems, focusing on the SS5 and A24 corridors in Italy.

The study brings together landscape analysis, sequential visual perception, ecological integration and spatial experience to examine relationships between infrastructure and its surroundings.

Research approach and scope

Territorial analysis: examining the relationship between roads and the wider landscape.

Sequential perception: investigating how landscape experience unfolds along a route.

Spatial interpretation: understanding roads as part of landscape systems.

This project is research and analysis in progress. It is not presented as a completed design intervention.

Background

Design experience.
Research perspective.

My background combines architectural design and computer modelling in practice in Iran with doctoral research in Italy. My interests include landscape design, environmental psychology, urban planning and the relationship between people and nature.

I worked in architectural design and computer modelling at Jahan Pardis in Tabriz from 2016 to 2023, following an internship at Isak in Tehran.
